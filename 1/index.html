[DRAFT] Same Concept, Different Directions

Recover features first. Align them afterward.

Chungpa Lee · Updated Oct 29, 2026

Mechanistic interpretability studies a model’s internal computations. The linear representation hypothesis proposes that concepts correspond to directions in representation space. [3]

A sparse autoencoder (SAE) learns feature directions whose sparse combinations reconstruct embeddings—the model’s vector representations. [4]
